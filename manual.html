Main screens

Rumbo has three screens: Podcasts, Playlists and Subscribe. Swipe left or right to move between them, or pick one from the title at the top (▾). Back from Playlists or Subscribe returns to Podcasts. Refresh, Settings, Help, About and Close Rumbo are in the ⋮ menu.

Podcasts

Your podcasts as a grid of covers; tap one to open it. Pull down to look for new episodes.

Topics

Turn on Topic tabs in Settings to group your podcasts in tabs. All always shows every podcast. Tap a tab to switch; + creates a topic.

Long-press a tab for: Columns (2–5), Sort (Alphabetical, Latest episode, Manual), Rename, Colour, Move left/right, Merge into… (its podcasts go to another topic and it is deleted) and Delete. All only has Columns and Sort. Each tab remembers its own.

To choose a podcast’s topics, long-press its cover, or use Topics… in its options. A podcast can be in several topics.

In Manual order, long-press a cover and move it to reorder; release without moving to choose its topics.

Organize by category (Settings) proposes one topic per category of your podcasts. Rename, untick or merge them, then Apply. Your current topics are kept.

Podcast and episodes

Inside a podcast, the small triangle at the top opens its options: hide played episodes, show only downloaded ones, order, auto-download of new episodes, Topics…, mark all as played and unsubscribe.

Tap an episode to see it: Play, Download (or delete the download), Add to playlist and Mark as played.

Searching episodes

In a podcast, tap 🔍 and type: the list filters as you write. It looks in titles and descriptions, ignoring capitals and accents (“cancion” finds “canción”), works without a connection, and searches all the saved episodes of that podcast, even played ones, whatever its filters. Close the search with ✕ or Back to see the normal list again.

Note: it only finds the episodes the feed provides.

Playlists

Downloaded lists every downloaded episode. Clips holds the clips you save. Tap + to create your own playlists; long-press a tab to rename, recolour, move or delete it.

Each list has its order and a Shuffle button. In Manual order, drag ≡ to reorder. An episode that is not downloaded shows in grey with ⬇️ to download it again. ✕ removes an episode from the list (Undo in the message).

New

Playlists › New lists the episodes found by updates in the last 7 days that you have not played, newest first. An episode leaves when you start playing it, mark it as played, remove it with ✕ (not marked as played) or after 7 days. Clear empties the list (Undo in the message). Episodes added when you subscribe do not count. After an update, View in the message opens this list.

Player

Controls: previous episode, skip back, play/pause, skip forward, next episode (the skip amounts are set in Settings). Previous and next follow the list you started playing from, and each episode continues where you left it. Tap or drag the thin bar at the top of the player to jump. Tap the title to open the episode. When an episode ends it is marked as played and, in continuous mode, the next one of the list plays. With less than 30 s left an episode counts as played. On Bluetooth headphones, next and previous skip forward and back.

Player options

Drag the player up, or tap ⌃ next to the title, to show these options. They light up when active.

  • Playback speed. Speed and the two sound options below are remembered for each podcast.
  • Skip silences: shortens pauses in the audio.
  • Volume boost for quiet episodes: tap for +4 dB, again for +8 dB, again to turn it off.
  • Sleep timer: stops playback after some minutes or at the end of the episode, fading out.
  • Continuous: when an episode ends, the next one of the list plays. Tap to change the mode.
  • Stop after this episode.
  • Repeat this episode (marked as played only once).
  • Delete after playing: removes the download of each episode you finish.
  • Make a clip of the episode (see Clips below).

Clips

Tap ✂️ in the player options (downloaded episodes only). The clip starts 30 s before the current position. Adjust start and end with −5 s / +5 s, or Here to use the current position (the other end moves if needed). Listen, then Save clip. Clips (up to 5 min) are in Playlists › Clips: play, share or delete them.

Subscribe

Search podcasts by name and tap +, or paste a feed (RSS) address.

Suggestions shows the top podcasts of each category; For you shows popular ones from your categories that you do not follow yet. The country button (🇪🇸 ES ▾) changes the country of the suggestions.

Import and Export move your subscriptions to or from other apps with an OPML file. You can also open or share an .opml file with Rumbo.

Settings

  • Language: the app’s own or the system’s.
  • Playback: default speed, skip silences and volume boost; skip back and forward amounts; pause when headphones are unplugged.
  • Podcasts: topic tabs, Organize by category, and columns and order when topic tabs are off.
  • Feeds: daily update on or off and its time.
  • Downloads: Wi-Fi only, storage used and delete all downloads.
  • Backup & Restore: export everything to a rumbo-backup.zip file, import one (it replaces everything in Rumbo) and a weekly copy in a folder you choose. You can also open or share a backup file with Rumbo.

Downloads

Downloaded episodes work without a connection. Downloads continue in the background and resume after a lost connection. By default they wait for Wi-Fi (see Settings).

Streaming

Episodes that are not downloaded can be played too: they stream and start downloading at the same time, and what you hear is kept so it is not downloaded twice. With Download only on Wi-Fi, on mobile data Rumbo asks first (Don't ask again turns on Streaming on mobile data in Settings); the rest of the download waits for Wi-Fi. Without a connection, only downloaded episodes play. Continuous playback includes episodes that are not downloaded only when they can be streamed at that moment; otherwise it skips them.

Updates

Feeds are checked once a day at the time set in Settings (it can be turned off), and when you tap refresh. Podcasts with auto-download on get their new episodes downloaded.

About

The ⋮ menu › About shows the version, the website, this manual and the privacy policy (they open in your browser), a way to write to the developer, Buy me a coffee, and the open-source licenses of the libraries Rumbo uses.

Closing Rumbo

Close Rumbo, in the ⋮ menu, stops playback (keeping your position) and downloads. Queued downloads continue the next time you open the app.
