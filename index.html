Rumbo

A podcast player for Android that is private, offline-ready and free of accounts, analytics and ads.

Buy me a coffee

What Rumbo does

  • Subscribe your waySearch by name, paste a feed (RSS) address or import your subscriptions with an OPML file. Suggestions show the top podcasts of each category and country.
  • PlaylistsDownloaded, New and Clips, plus your own playlists. Shuffle them and keep the order you want.
  • TopicsGroup your podcasts in colour tabs. Rumbo can propose topics from the categories of your podcasts.
  • Listen your waySpeed for each podcast, skip silences, volume boost, sleep timer, continuous, stop or repeat modes, and delete after playing.
  • ClipsCut a piece of an episode (up to 5 minutes) and share it.
  • Offline first, streaming tooDownloaded episodes work without a connection (downloads wait for Wi-Fi by default). Episodes you have not downloaded can be streamed.
  • Find episodesSearch the titles and descriptions of a podcast's episodes, also without a connection.
  • Backup & restoreExport everything to a file you control and restore it later, with an optional weekly copy in a folder you choose.
  • In your languageEnglish, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese (Brazil).
